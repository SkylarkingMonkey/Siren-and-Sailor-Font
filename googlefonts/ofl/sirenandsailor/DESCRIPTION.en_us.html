Siren and Sailor is a nautical display serif designed by Jean-Marc Daecius. Swept serifs, sharp hook terminals, barbs and pronounced stroke contrast give its letters the character of hand-drawn maritime lettering.

The family includes uppercase and lowercase letters, proportional and tabular numerals, Latin Core accents, punctuation and currency symbols. It is intended for titles, covers, signage and other large-format settings.

To contribute, see github.com/SkylarkingMonkey/Siren-and-Sailor-Font.
