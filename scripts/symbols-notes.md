# Supplemental symbols

`symbols.py:add_symbols(font)` adds 45 uniquely mapped symbols and replaces the two parentheses under the user's later design instructions. It returns 47 names. No third-party font outlines are used.

- `$` keeps its approved S-derived body, with two continuous shafts and two outward-facing harpoons from the actual approved capital-J hook.
- `%` carries the same J hook at both ends of its diagonal. Its oval counters remain readable.
- Each parenthesis preserves the original middle arc; its top and bottom use outward J-hook barbs. Attachment positions and widths are calculated from the clipped original contours, with a short blended neck.
- `*` is a small six-point nautical star; `#` uses the matching restrained flared crossbars.
- Other currency and enclosed-letter symbols reuse approved cap outlines where appropriate; the custom rupee has two horizontal bars and a continuous bowl-to-diagonal junction.
- Curly quotes derive from the approved comma. Inverted exclamation/question signs preserve the original shapes with their dots at x-height.

Final production removes overlaps and normalizes mathematical advances in the main builder. A temporary proof rendered from a compiled TTF was visually inspected after the J-hook updates. All mapped glyphs have positive advances and drawable contours. The only replaced approved base glyphs are the explicitly authorized parentheses.
